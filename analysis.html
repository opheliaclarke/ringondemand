4. The tracking gap (why this matters most)

Ringba shows the campaigns are generating qualified calls (6–15 calls of 60s+ per day). Google's conversion column sees almost none of it after Jun 16.

Because Google can't see the conversions, Maximize Conversions is optimizing blind — which is exactly why it overbids. Fixing attribution is the root cause, not a side issue:

  • gclid → Ringba handoff is failing on some calls (your "blank, no-gclid" call). If the landing page's dynamic number insertion doesn't capture & pass gclid, Ringba can't upload that conversion back to Google.
  • Denver shows "Eligible (Misconfigured): website missing a Google tag."
  • Calls placed to the number shown directly in the ad / on the site are inherently untrackable — accepted, but those are separate from the click→call gap above.

5. What to change — revised

You pushed back on dropping Maximize Conversions — and you're right. It's what started producing calls, and crashing to $20–40 manual bids would lose the high-intent auctions. So: keep it. Fix it surgically instead.

Priority 1 — Fix conversion tracking first

Get Google seeing the ~2 qualified calls/day Ringba already logs. Verify every landing page captures gclid and passes it into the Ringba dynamic number; confirm Ringba's Google offline-conversion upload is firing; fix Denver's missing Google tag. Once Google sees real conversions, smart bidding rationalizes on its own.

Priority 2 — Add a Target CPA to Maximize Conversions

Set a Target CPA around $100–130 on the existing strategy. This keeps conversion-based bidding on the high-intent terms but tells Google not to chase a $196 click (which implies a CPA far above target). It's the cap that stops the bleed without killing reach. If volume throttles too hard after ~1 week, raise the target.

Priority 3 — Make the $500/day cap real

Per-campaign budgets don't cap the total, and Max-Conversions can spend 2× on any day. Options:

  • One shared budget of $500/day across all 7 campaigns (Tools → Shared library → Budgets → +). Caps the monthly average at ~$500/day (~$15.2k/mo); single days can still flex to ~2×, averaging out.
  • For a hard daily ceiling (no day over $500): set the shared budget to $250/day — the 2× rule then tops out at ~$500. Lower volume, but a true cap.
  • Trade-off: a shared budget lets Google shift spend to the best performers but removes per-city control.

Priority 4 — Tighten match types

Set the proven call-drivers (e.g. plumbers near me, plumber [city]) to phrase/exact so the budget isn't spent on loose broad matches at top dollar.
