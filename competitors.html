RingOnDemand — Where We Sit in the Plumbing Market

A one-page explainer: our business model, who we really compete with, and what to share with the Google Ads team.

🧭 What business are we actually in?

We are NOT a plumbing company. We don't fix pipes and we don't have crews.

We are a pay-per-call lead generator (affiliate). We run Google Ads on city landing pages → a homeowner with a plumbing problem calls → that call is tracked (Ringba) → we sell the qualified call to a contractor or a lead-buying network. We only get paid when a real, qualified call happens.

⚔️ The competition comes in two layers

In any plumbing search auction on Google, there are two different kinds of bidders. They look the same to Google, but they have opposite business models:

Layer A — the service brands

Companies that want to do the plumbing job

National brands with their own trucks, teams and offices across the US.

Roto-Rooter · Mr. Rooter · Benjamin Franklin · ARS/Rescue Rooter · bluefrog

Layer B — the lead aggregators

Companies that want to sell the call ← THIS IS US

They buy ad traffic, generate the call/lead, and resell it to contractors. Same model as RingOnDemand.

Service Direct · eLocal · 33 Mile Radius · Networx · Angi Leads (HomeAdvisor)

Why this matters: Both layers bid on the same words ("emergency plumber", "drain cleaning"). Everyone in Layer B is buying the same calls to resell them, which is a big reason the first-page bids are so high — and why our keywords show "Eligible (limited)". We're competing against resellers AND national brands for the same clicks.
