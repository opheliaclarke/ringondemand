⛔ The "limited by budget" story is wrong — and that's good news

Every campaign spends only 62–85% of its daily budget (West Covina: 0%). They can't even spend what they already have. The bottleneck is bid suppression → rank, not budget — we're losing 53–83% of impressions to Ad Rank, vs mostly <15% to budget. Raising budgets right now would burn more money for the same ~0 reported calls. The real fix is cheaper and bigger: turn the conversion feedback loop back on.

The one number that explains everything: Ringba recorded 68 qualified (60s+) calls in 14 days (~146/month). Google's call-import conversion action RB-plumbing imported ZERO of them. Google Smart Bidding is optimizing on 4 conversions when ~68 real qualified calls already happened. The calls ARE coming — Google just can't see them, so it refuses to bid to get more.

🔗 Root-cause chain

  1. Conversion feed is broken. RB-plumbing (call import) = 0 in 14 days. Only ~7 sparse GCLID uploads got through.
  2. Smart Bidding is starved — and tCPA $90 is mathematically impossible when a single click costs $68–146. You'd need >1 call per click.
  3. So the algorithm suppresses bids to "protect" the $90 target.
  4. Suppressed bids lose the auction → 53–83% of impressions lost to Ad Rank.
  5. Tiny impression volume → few clicks → few calls Google can even attribute.
  6. Fewer conversions seen → bidding gets more conservative → the spiral tightens.

Note: the tCPA $90 target went live Jun 19 — qualified-call volume dropped right after (14→1→3 calls Jun 18→19→20). It's actively choking delivery.
