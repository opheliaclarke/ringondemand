Strategy shift — live now

2026-06-22 · Account: Plumber Elite (9571863699) · ← dashboard

Why we changed: in click-to-call plumbing, people dial straight from the ad — neither our number pool nor Google can reliably track those calls. So conversion-based bidding (tCPA) was flying blind and choking delivery. We've stopped chasing untrackable data and switched to a model that just buys the most calls per dollar, focused only on the cities that ring.

🔧 The 3 changes we made

  • Paused 4 cities — West Covina (was dead, $0 spent), Boulder, Denver, Portland (no tracked calls / too expensive).
  • Switched bidding from "Target CPA $90" → Maximize Clicks with a $30 cap per click (stops $100+ click waste, no longer needs conversion tracking).
  • Re-allocated the budget onto the 3 cities that actually ring: Littleton $180, Boise $170, Santa Barbara $150.

📞 How we judge it (important)

  • Only calls on our own numbers list count. Ignore every other call in Ringba — they are not ours and do not count toward performance.
  • Watch over the next 1–2 weeks: are the 3 cities getting more calls on our numbers for the same $500/day?
  • If a city dries up → pause it and move its budget to the others. If one is on fire → give it more.
