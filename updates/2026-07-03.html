What the fuck is happening — 4 things at once

1. Google is bidding BLIND. Only 1 call ever imported as a conversion in Google (the RB-plumbing call feed is broken). So Google can't optimize toward calls — it just spends the budget at whatever CPC the auction demands. That's how Jun 29 burned $861 in one day for zero qualified calls.
2. The economics are underwater. ~$1,680 spent → 3 ad-confirmed calls / 3 qualified calls on ad-days = roughly $560 per good call. Emergency-plumbing CPCs are $48–62, so a big budget buys very few clicks, and only a fraction become qualified calls.
3. The buyer's funnel kills the good ones. We proved the traffic is real — a 155s Boise call and a 223s Denver call connected and held on Jun 30. But the same week: an agent told a Denver sewer-repair caller it's an "answering service" (33s, lost); an IVR zip-code gate dropped a caller at 9s; and STIR/SHAKEN blocked 3 Littleton calls before they rang. Those are all on Leo's Call-Tools/Ringba side.
4. Organic muddies the read. On $0-spend days (Jun 22, 23, Jul 2) calls still landed on our numbers — those are organic Google-Business-Profile calls, not from our ads. So the raw "calls on our numbers" overstates what the ads actually produced.

Bottom line

  • It's not "no calls" — it's very few QUALIFIED calls, and Google can't see them. 16 connected calls in two weeks, only 4 held 60s+, only 3 tied back to an ad click.
  • $1,680 for 3 good calls is unsustainable — and $861 of it went out in a single blind-bidding day.
  • The two things that must be fixed before spending again: (a) repair the Google call-conversion feed so bidding isn't blind; (b) fix Leo's call handling (agent script, IVR gate, STIR filter) so the calls we do buy don't die on pickup.
  • With the deposit unpaid and the campaign paused, we've stopped a bleeding account — not lost a winning one.
